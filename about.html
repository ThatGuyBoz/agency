About Us

We're obsessed
with your growth.

Mirenzo Media is an independent creative marketing agency born in Brisbane — built by strategists, designers, and data scientists who refused to do average work.

We started because
most agencies settle.

Founded in Brisbane in 2019 by a group of marketers tired of cookie-cutter campaigns and vanity metrics, Mirenzo Media was built around a simple idea: your growth is a craft, and it deserves to be treated like one.

Today we work with brands across e-commerce, SaaS, retail, hospitality, and finance — from early-stage Australian startups to established companies looking to reclaim market share. Our team of 28 spans strategy, creative, media, and technology.

We don't pitch and disappear. Every client gets a dedicated team, weekly strategy sessions, and transparent reporting tied directly to revenue outcomes — not impressions.

We're proud to call Brisbane home. The city is one of Australia's fastest-growing business hubs, and we've built our reputation on helping local and national brands punch well above their weight.
